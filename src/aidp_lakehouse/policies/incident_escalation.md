# Fictional incident escalation procedure

## 1. Scope

This fictional procedure supports the synthetic operations demo. The response
times below are illustrative internal targets, not real service commitments.

## 2. Triage and recording

Open an incident when an operational exception threatens an accepted delivery
promise. Record the order key, observation time, exception type, impact and
responsible coordinator. Link subsequent updates to the same incident.

## 3. Severity and acknowledgement

Severity 1 covers safety concerns or a site-wide interruption: notify the duty
manager immediately and target acknowledgement within 15 minutes. Severity 2
covers a threatened customer commitment: notify the team lead and target one
hour. Severity 3 covers a contained issue: review within one business day.

## 4. Escalation and communication

Escalate one level when acknowledgement is overdue or impact increases. The
coordinator owns customer communication; a model or assistant must not send
messages or promise a revised delivery date without a person's approval.

## 5. Closure and learning

Close only after verifying delivery or an approved alternative. Retain incident
history and original timestamps. Resolution, dispatch and delivery information
is unavailable at order acceptance and is excluded from acceptance-time features.
